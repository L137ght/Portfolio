# Atribe Product Requirements

## Product Purpose
Atribe allows users to support creators when shopping online by routing product links through creator-affiliate or brand-campaign flows.

## Key Capabilities
- Works across multiple brands, not just Amazon
- Creators can add affiliate links per brand/domain
- Brands can connect Shopify and launch creator campaigns
- Shoppers can preview attribution before opening a product link

## Core Themes
- Community and creator support
- Clear product routing and attribution
- Low cognitive load
- Mobile-first task flows
- Brand / creator / shopper role switching

## Design Goals
- Clean, minimal, modern, and dark
- Trustworthy and slightly premium
- Compact layouts that still read well on mobile
- Strong focus on input → output clarity
