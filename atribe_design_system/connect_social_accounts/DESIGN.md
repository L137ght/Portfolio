## Overview
Atribe’s screen renders are now aligned to the current product surface: shopper routing, creator tooling, brand onboarding, and campaign management. Keep the copy short, role-aware, and mobile-safe.

## Do’s and Don’ts
Do:
- Use the current route names and current product copy
- Keep layouts dark, compact, and mobile-first
- Prefer cards, chips, and stacked sections over long editorial pages
- Show support, creator, and brand flows with the same visual language

Don’t:
- Reintroduce old login variants or duplicate role-specific home screens
- Use outdated “Nocturne editorial” prose or placeholder copy
- Keep screens that no longer exist in the app
