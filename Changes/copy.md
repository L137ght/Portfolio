## Portfolio Offering
Independent design shop focused on product systems across web, mobile, and commerce.

Create product experiences that begin with a strong landing view and expand into the full working system behind it.

### Capability Cards
- Web UI Design
  React, Vite, TypeScript interfaces for dashboards, product flows, and marketing surfaces.
- Mobile Product Design
  iOS and Android experiences designed for touch-first onboarding, browsing, transactions, and retention.
- Database-Aware Product Thinking
  Data models, content structures, and admin relationships considered as part of product design.
- Shopify Apps
  Commerce tooling, storefront extensions, and merchant-facing workflows.
- CLI Tools
  Internal tools and command-line workflows for operators, creators, and product teams.

- Web Apps
- Native Desktop Apps
- iOS Apps
- Android Apps
